An easily frightened rabbit and a persistent lizard knocked themselves out after crashing into a seemingly empty building!

How'd that happen?

Well, from the very beginning our bunny, gecko, and feline friend had received news that they'd been elected to go out of town to get supplies for their home, "Able" an orphanage built inside the remains of an old beached cargo ship.

While receiving the briefing, the lizard spooked the rabbit and started a chase! The bunny, fearing for the worst; ran with all they had across Able, eventually running into a fox and dog named "Yit" and "Damian" respectively. But they only seemed to interested in getting some coin off of our rattled rabbit. Being in one place too long would spell an end to this "game" of tag, allowing this relentless reptile to catch up.

Out of desperation, or love of the game, the lizard tackles the rabbit crashing them where they are now...

What are these critter's names anyway? And what does the lizard actually even want?
